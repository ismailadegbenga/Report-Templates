## Cholelithiasis

Magnetic Resonance Cholangiopancreatography
Technique: Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatogram using T2W, T2-SPAIR, dual-FFE, Diffusion-weighted and contrast-enhanced sequences at 1.5T
Findings:- 
The gallbladder is moderately dilated. There are multiple (50 - 100) round hypointense filling defects, consistent with calculi, within the gall bladder. Each of this calculi with a typical diameter of about 5 mm. There is no gallbladder wall thickening or pericholecystic fat stranding. The intrahepatic biliary ducts, cystic duct, common hepatic duct, pancreatic ducts and, common bile duct are not dilated. There is no mass lesion in the pancreas. The liver and spleen are normal is size. There is no hepatic mass lesion. The portal and hepatic veins are normal. There is no ascites or lymphadenopathy. The kidneys and adrenal glands are normal. 
Impression: Cholecystolithiasis.
